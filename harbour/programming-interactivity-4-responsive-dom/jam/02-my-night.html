Market jam · task 2

My night

A ♡ on every stall. Save the ones you want to visit, see how many you saved, and show only those. ★★☆

“Forty stalls. I want the five I care about, and I want them still there when I open the page again on my phone at the market.”

You'll demo: tap ♡ on two stalls: they turn ♥, their cards are marked and the header says ♥ 2. Tick "only my saved stalls": the others disappear. Reload: still saved.

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

HTML · index.html

  • A save button on every stall. A screen reader knows which stall it saves and whether it's saved.
  • A saved-count in the header, and a switch for "only my saved stalls".

CSS · css/feature.css

  • A saved stall looks saved: the button and the whole card.
  • In only-saved mode, the other stalls are gone.

JS · js/feature.js

  • Saving and unsaving works on every stall with one listener.
  • The count is always right.
  • Saved stalls are still saved after a reload.

Before you start: js/market.js already declares stalls, form, nav, filters, count. Declaring one again in feature.js is a SyntaxError ("has already been declared") and none of your file runs. Pick your own names.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • The counter link scrolls to the vendors and turns on only-saved mode.
  • An empty state: "Nothing saved yet: tap a ♡".
  • Works together with the Food/Music filter: what happens if both hide things?

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first. The tools are on the toolbox slide and in the cheat sheet (box 12).

Hint 1

Which attribute tells a screen reader that a toggle button is on? You used it on the menu in Exercise 3.

Hint 2

Can CSS style a card because of a button inside it?

Hint 3

What do you store, exactly? It has to mean the same thing after a reload, when every element is new.

Hint 4

localStorage stores strings only. A list goes in with JSON.stringify and comes out with JSON.parse.

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • The Food/Music filter already hides stalls with the hidden attribute. If you use it too, each feature undoes the other.
  • Cards have a "Sold out" stamp in the top-right corner.
