Market jam · spare task (for a sixth group, or a group that finishes early)

Register a stall

A form under the vendors: a new stall fills in its name, category and one line, and its card appears in the list, filters and all. ★★★

“We sell tacos from a bike. The market is full, but someone cancelled: can we get on the page tonight, without emailing anyone?”

You'll demo: open "＋ Register your stall", fill it in, press Add: a new card glows at the end of the vendor list, in the right category, and the form empties. Try a name that already exists: the browser refuses it, with your message. Then click "Food": is the new food stall still there? (It should be. Find out why it isn't.)

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

HTML · index.html

  • A collapsible "＋ Register your stall" section under the vendors, without JavaScript.
  • A form: name (required, up to 40), category (food, music, crafts, drinks), one line (required, up to 120). A status line screen readers announce.

CSS · css/feature.css

  • Two columns from ~40rem, one on a phone.
  • Errors show only after someone tried.
  • The new card glows for a moment, only without a reduced-motion wish.

JS · js/feature.js

  • Add puts a new card at the end of the list, styled exactly like the others, with their words inserted safely.
  • A name already on the page is refused, with your message in the browser's bubble.
  • The status confirms it and the form empties.

Before you start: js/market.js already declares stalls, form, nav, filters, count. Declaring one again in feature.js is a SyntaxError ("has already been declared") and none of your file runs. Pick your own names.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • Make the Food/Music filter see your new stalls (you may edit js/market.js for this one).
  • Remember registered stalls in localStorage and rebuild them on load.
  • Scroll the new card into view, and move keyboard focus to it.

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first. The tools are on the toolbox slide and in the cheat sheet (box 12).

Hint 1

Which HTML element collapses and expands on its own?

Hint 2

Look at an existing card in index.html. A copy needs the same elements and classes, built from the inside out.

Hint 3

The category's value is "food", the card says "Food". A select knows its selectedOptions.

Hint 4

A field can get a rule HTML can't write: input.setCustomValidity(message); an empty string means valid.

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • The filter can't see your new stall. Read how js/market.js finds the stalls, and when. Explain it on stage: it's the most useful bug of the night.
  • A card with a different structure won't get the styles or the stamp position.
