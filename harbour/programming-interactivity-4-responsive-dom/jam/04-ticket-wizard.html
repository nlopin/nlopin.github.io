Market jam · task 4

Ticket wizard

The ticket form, one step at a time: You → Your visit → Check. A progress strip on top, and a review of every answer before Reserve. ★★★

“On my phone the ticket form is one endless column, and I only find out at the very end that my email was wrong. Three short steps, please, and let me see everything before I book.”

You'll demo: step 1 asks for name and email; Next refuses until both are valid, with the browser's own message. Step 2: guests, arrival, note. Step 3 lists every answer. Back works. Reserve books the ticket (market.js already does that) and the form starts again at step 1.

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

HTML · index.html

  • The existing form, split into three steps: You (name, email) · Your visit (the rest) · Check (a review). Same form, same fields: market.js still books the ticket.
  • A progress strip that says which step you're on, to screen readers too.
  • Back and Next.

CSS · css/feature.css

  • One step visible at a time, each with the form's two-column layout from ~40rem.
  • Done, current and upcoming steps look different in the strip, with no JavaScript for the looks.
  • The review is easy to scan: label, answer.

JS · js/feature.js

  • Next won't leave a step with an invalid field, and shows the browser's message on that field.
  • The review lists every answer, inserted safely.
  • Enter can't book a ticket from step 1.
  • After booking, the wizard starts again at step 1.

Before you start: js/market.js already declares stalls, form, nav, filters, count. Declaring one again in feature.js is a SyntaxError ("has already been declared") and none of your file runs. Pick your own names.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • Enter in a field goes to the next step.
  • Focus moves to the first field of each new step, and to Reserve on the last: try it with the keyboard only.
  • An "Edit" button next to each answer in the review jumps back to its step.

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first. The tools are on the toolbox slide and in the cheat sheet (box 12).

Hint 1

Which element groups form fields and has a caption of its own? It also has an .elements list.

Hint 2

Every field can check itself: checkValidity() and reportValidity().

Hint 3

Write one function that draws the whole wizard for step i. Every button just calls it.

Hint 4

CSS can count: counter-reset, counter-increment, counter().

Hint 5

form.reset() fires a reset event.

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • What does a <button> inside a form do when you don't give it a type?
  • Wrapping fields in a new element breaks the form's grid from Lecture 3: the grid items changed.
  • Enter in a text field submits the form: "implicit submission". Find out when the browser does it and when it doesn't.
  • market.js already listens for submit and builds the ticket. Your script only manages the steps.
