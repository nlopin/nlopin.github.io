Market jam · task 3

What's on?

A time slider above the schedule: pick a time, see what's on, and the schedule fits a phone. ★★☆

“It's 22:40 and I'm by the drinks. Is the brass band still playing? The table is too wide for my phone anyway.”

You'll demo: drag the slider to 22:30: "Harbour Brass Band · The pier" appears and that row lights up. At phone width, the table turns into a list of small cards with labels.

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

HTML · index.html

  • A labelled slider from 19:00 to 01:00 in half hours, the chosen time written next to it, and a line for what's on.

CSS · css/feature.css

  • The act that's on is highlighted in the schedule.
  • Below ~40rem the table becomes a stack of small cards, and every value keeps its label (Time, What, Where).

JS · js/feature.js

  • Dragging updates the time, the line and the highlight, while you drag.
  • Correct after midnight: at 00:30 the silent disco is on.
  • Times show as "22:30", never "22.5".

Before you start: js/market.js already declares stalls, form, nav, filters, count. Declaring one again in feature.js is a SyntaxError ("has already been declared") and none of your file runs. Pick your own names.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • A "Now" button that sets the slider to the real time.
  • "Starts in 25 minutes" for the next act.
  • The highlighted row scrolls into view on a phone.

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first. The tools are on the toolbox slide and in the cheat sheet (box 12).

Hint 1

Which event fires while you drag: input or change?

Hint 2

A slider works with numbers; the schedule has text like "00:30". How do you turn it into a number that comes after 22:30?

Hint 3

CSS can print a value it reads from the element itself: look up attr() inside content.

Hint 4

row.cells is the list of a table row's cells.

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • Every form value is a string, the slider's too.
  • A table row with display: block loses its table layout: that's the point on a phone, and a bug on a laptop.
