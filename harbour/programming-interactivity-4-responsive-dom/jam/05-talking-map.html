Market jam · task 5

The map talks

Click a zone on the market map and a panel tells you what's there. On a phone, the panel slides up from the bottom. ★★☆

“The map is pretty, but what is “Crafts corner”? Who's there, and what's on?”

You'll demo: click "The pier": it gets an outline and a panel says "The pier · Harbour Brass Band at 22:30…". Click another zone: the panel changes. ✕ or Escape closes it. At phone width it's a bottom sheet.

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

HTML · index.html

  • Every zone works with a mouse and with the keyboard alone (Tab, Enter).
  • A panel for the zone's name and what's on, with a close button. Hidden until you pick a zone.

CSS · css/feature.css

  • The zones still look like the map after your HTML change.
  • The selected zone is clearly marked.
  • Below ~40rem the panel is a bottom sheet; above, it sits under the map.

JS · js/feature.js

  • Pick a zone: the panel shows its name and text. Pick another: the panel changes.
  • One listener for all seven zones.
  • ✕ and Escape close the panel and clear the mark.

Before you start: js/market.js already declares stalls, form, nav, filters, count. Declaring one again in feature.js is a SyntaxError ("has already been declared") and none of your file runs. Pick your own names.

Data to paste

The keys match the zones' classes:

const ZONES = {
  stage:  ["Main stage", "DJ Marisol at 19:00, Los Faroles at 20:30."],
  food:   ["Food street", "Dumpling Dynasty, Churros Till Late and twelve more."],
  table:  ["Long table", "Sixty seats. Dumpling folding class at 19:30."],
  crafts: ["Crafts corner", "Salt & Ink, The Plant Swap, zines at 21:30."],
  drinks: ["Drinks", "Mezcal Moon, lemonade, tea. Last orders 00:30."],
  pier:   ["The pier", "Harbour Brass Band at 22:30, silent disco at midnight."],
  gate:   ["Entrance", "Moll de la Fusta. Metro: Drassanes."],
};

Stretch, if you're done early

  • The sheet slides up (an animation), only without a reduced-motion wish.
  • List the vendors in that zone, read from the cards (give each .stall a data-zone).
  • Focus moves to the panel when it opens, and back to the zone when it closes.

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first. The tools are on the toolbox slide and in the cheat sheet (box 12).

Hint 1

Which element is clickable and reachable with Tab, without extra work?

Hint 2

Every zone has the class zone and one more. The other one is a key of ZONES. How do you find it?

Hint 3

Escape anywhere on the page: which element do you listen on?

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • The "You are here" pin sits on top of the gate. Click it: what did your listener get?
  • A fixed panel at the bottom of the screen shares that corner with the back-to-top button.
