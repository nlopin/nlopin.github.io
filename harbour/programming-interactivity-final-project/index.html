Final project

Explain one idea

Build a playground or a demo that explains an idea or a concept, or that compares several approaches to the same problem. How you show it is your call. On demo day you show it working, then you explain what happens under the hood.

Key dates

  • Friday 9 October · end of daySend me your topicIn Slack: the number from the list below, or your own topic in one or two sentences.
  • Thursday 15 OctoberSend the link to your repo via ClassroomI read your code that evening and prepare questions about it.
  • Friday 16 OctoberDemo dayPresent your work and answer technical questions on how you built it.

The rule

AI is allowed. You must be able to explain every line.

The questions come from your repository. I can point to any line and ask what it does, why it's there, and what happens if it's removed. "The AI wrote it" is not an answer.

On demo day

  1. The demo. Show the idea working. Your audience should understand it by the end.
  2. Under the hood. How one piece works: the algorithm, the formula, how the data is transformed.
  3. Questions. Technical questions about your implementation, prepared from your repo.
