Exercise 2 · Live map

You're a courier in Barcelona. Your page sends your position to the dispatcher over a WebSocket; a customer follows your order over server-sent events. You write the courier's side of the conversation.

Start

⤓ Download the starterThe dispatcher map ↗
cd 02-live-map
npx live-server --port=8080
  • courier.html: your courier. A WebSocket to wss://fleet.lopin.me/courier.
  • track.html: the customer's page. An EventSource on one order.
  • track.html is complete. courier.js has the map, the order display and the timer; three functions are empty, each with a TODO.

Steps

  1. connect(name): open a WebSocket to SERVER, send the hello once it's open, pass every message to handle(), and when it closes, stop driving and show the close code.
  2. handle(message): one branch per message the server sends down: welcome, order, status, message, error. The table below says what each one carries.
  3. step(): once a second, move your marker to the next point of the route and send that point to the server as a position.
  4. Open courier.html, enter your name, start your shift. Find yourself on the projector. Note your order id.

Done means

  • The status line says you're connected, as your name.
  • Your marker drives the route on your map, and your courier drives the same way on the projector.
  • The order status changes: to the restaurant, to the customer, delivered. A few seconds later, a new order starts.

Check it

  • DevTools → Network → courier → Messages: every frame, both directions. Which one is your hello, which is the welcome?
  • Change SERVER to a wrong URL and reload: which close code do you get?
  • Open your courier in a second tab. What happens to the first one, and why?

The protocol

JSON text messages. The first one must be a hello.

directionmessagemeaning
up{ "type": "hello", "name": "Ana" }a new courier
up{ "type": "position", "lat": 41.38, "lon": 2.17 }where you are
up{ "type": "message", "text": "…" }to the customer
down{ "type": "welcome", "courierId", "name", "order", "resumeAt" }your id, your order, and the route index to start from
down{ "type": "order", "order" }the next order, 3 s after a delivery
down{ "type": "status", "status" }picked_up, then delivered
down{ "type": "message", "text" }from the customer
close1012the server restarted
close4000the same courier connected from somewhere else
