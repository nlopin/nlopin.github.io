Six frames

Exercise 2 · Sketch what's on screen; write one sentence under each frame.

The idea, as one sentence with a verb
Name

1 · The question what the reader sees first, why they care

2 · The first touch one control; what happens when it moves

3 · The second control the "but" / "therefore" that brings it in

4 · The surprise and the default that makes it happen

5 · The rule the general statement, in words

6 · The ending a question, or a sandbox

Your neighbour answers

  1. What question does it open with? Would I care?
  2. How many seconds until I can touch something?
  3. Where is the "but" or "therefore" between frames 2 and 3?
  4. How does it end: a sandbox, a summary, a question?
