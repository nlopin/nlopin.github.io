One A4 page. Print one per group (Ctrl/⌘ + P), or copy the questions into a shared doc.

Dissection card

Exercise 1 · Take one apart. About the author's decisions, not the topic.

Piece
Group
  1. The idea in one sentence (a sentence with a verb, not a topic).
  2. What question does it open with? Did you care by the end of the first screen?
  3. Seconds until the first thing you can touch. What is it?
  4. Which controls exist at the start, which appear later?
  5. One "but" and one "therefore" in the sequence of sections.
  6. How many views of the same state? Are they linked?
  7. Where were you surprised? What made the surprise possible?
  8. Scroll through it again without touching anything. What's lost?
  9. How does it end: a sandbox, a summary, a question?
