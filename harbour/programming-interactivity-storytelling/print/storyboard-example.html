Six frames

Exercise 2 · example

Capacity caps a burst; the refill rate caps the average.
Example

1 · The question what the reader sees first, why they care

A client sends six requests at once; the API allows a burst of four. Guess first: buttons 0–6. Nothing else to touch.

2 · The first touch one control; what happens when it moves

Drag time across the burst: each request turns green or red as time reaches it; the bucket drains. Only the time slider.

3 · The second control the "but" / "therefore" that brings it in

But two later requests pass. Therefore tokens come back: the refill-rate slider appears. Slow it: 4.5 s fails.

4 · The surprise and the default that makes it happen

But after a long wait the same burst still loses two. Defaults: capacity 4, the same burst of 6, a quiet stretch before it.

5 · The rule the general statement, in words

Therefore capacity caps the burst: the capacity slider appears. The rule, in one sentence.

6 · The ending a question, or a sandbox

Twelve evenly spaced requests, a bucket of 3: the slowest rate that lets all through? Then bunch four together. Every control open.
