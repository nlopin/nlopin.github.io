    1. 
          
      Make every line show the meeting in that person's own time.
      
          
        
          
        A hint
        The second argument of toLocaleTimeString takes more options than hour and minute. Each person already has the value it needs.
        
          
        The answer
        meeting.toLocaleTimeString("en-GB", {
  hour: "2-digit", minute: "2-digit", timeZone: person.timeZone,
});

        Predict first. Move the meeting a week earlier: "2026-10-27T08:00:00Z", still 09:00 in Berlin. Which line will change? Write your guess down, then edit and look.
        
          
        The answer
        New York: 04:00 instead of 03:00. Europe left summer time on 25 October, the United States only on 1 November, so for one week Berlin is five hours ahead of New York, not six. Nobody finds that by reading the code.

        Predict first. Add Priya in Kolkata ("Asia/Kolkata"). What will her line say?
        
          
        The answer
        13:30. India is five and a half hours ahead of UTC, and doesn't change its clocks. Time zones aren't whole hours.
