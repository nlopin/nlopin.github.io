Harbour Night Market

FAQ

Is it really free?

Entry is free. Bring cash or a card for the stalls.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, on a leash. The dumplings are not for dogs.

What if it rains?

We move under the big tent next to the main stage. The market has never closed early.
