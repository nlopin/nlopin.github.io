Lecture 3

Layout, or where things go

Normal flow, position, flexbox and grid — four ways the browser places a box — taught on one page, the Harbour Night Market, which starts the day as a receipt and ends it with a floor plan.

▶ Open the slidesCheat sheetStarter project (zip)

Exercises in class order

  • Exercise 1 · Act 1 · 10 minFlow detectiveSeven layout bugs: sizing, margin collapse, intrinsic width, the cascade.
  • Exercise 2 · Act 2 · 12 minPin itStamps, a sticky menu, a map pin, stacking contexts, a trapped fixed button.
  • Exercise 3a · Act 3 · 8 min + homeworkStall SetterA layout game, 35 levels: Position, Flexbox, Grid and Deep cuts.
  • Exercise 3b · Act 3 · 10 minLine them upThe nav bar, the wobbly links, the centred hero, the automatic minimum.
  • Exercise 4 · Act 4 · 11 minDraw the floor planThe magic line, the map in words, subgrid, overlap without position.
  • Exercise 5 · finale · 15 min + homeworkOpen the marketEverything, in your own project: layout.css.
