Finale

Open the market

This is the recap of the lecture — by building it. You rebuild everything from today, act by act, in your own project: one file, css/layout.css. The look stays in css/outfit.css.

How to recap. Take one act at a time, in order. Write it from memory first, then compare with the showcase at that step (switcher 1–4). Only when you're stuck, reopen that act's exercise page: your solution is still there. For every box, ask: which algorithm places it, and who decides its width?

Set up (1 minute)

  1. Copy 03-layout/project/starter/ out of the course repo into your own folder (or unzip starter.zip there), and open that copy in your editor. Don't edit inside the repo: the next git pull would fight your changes. New in the HTML: a map, "Find the stall" links on the cards, a back-to-top button.
  2. Open css/layout.css. Every act has a block with its problems, the selectors you'll need and a "watch out" line.
  3. Keep the finished showcase open in a tab. Its switcher (0–4) shows the page act by act.

At home, optional: use your own look from last time. Copy your style.css into css/, point the first <link> at it, and delete its "layout magic" line. Expect a few cascade fights: that's Exercise 1, case 6.

Use the layout inspector

DevTools → Elements. Next to every flex or grid container there's a little flex / grid badge. Click it: the browser draws the lines, the gaps and the area names on the page. Firefox's grid inspector is even better.

When it doesn't work

SymptomUsual reason
My absolute element flew to the corner of the pageNo ancestor has position other than static. Put position: relative on the one you mean.
Sticky doesn't stickNo top value. Or its parent is only as tall as it is (sticky can't leave its parent). Or an ancestor has overflow: hidden.
justify-content does nothingYou put it on the item, not the flex container. Or you're in a column and wanted align-items. Or there's no free space to distribute (an item has flex: 1).
Flex items squash their textThey shrink by default. flex-shrink: 0 on the one that must keep its size, or flex-wrap: wrap on the container.
height: 100% does nothingThe parent's height is auto. Use min-height with vh, or let flex/grid stretch it.
My grid-template-areas is ignoredRows have different numbers of cells, an area isn't a rectangle, or a missing quote. One mistake invalidates the whole declaration: DevTools shows it crossed out with ⚠.
The layout rule is there but crossed outIt's losing the cascade. Your old style.css may have a more specific rule. Act 1, case 6.
Something sticks out sidewaysNo box-sizing reset, or a fixed width in px. Add the reset; prefer max-width.
