The green room, step by step

The screen a video-call app shows before you join: a camera preview, a microphone level, a device picker. Built as three custom elements, then connected to the real camera and microphone. Every step is a complete folder: open it, read the diff from the step before, run it.

Serve this folder over http://localhost: module scripts need http://, and from step 9 on the camera and microphone need localhost or HTTPS. Step 13 needs localhost and 127.0.0.1 on the same port, as two origins.

    The markup we'd write if HTML had the widgets. Unknown tags, no error, nothing rendered.

  2. 02Define

  6. 06Slots

    <camera-preview> with a default slot and a blocked slot for the app's own text; a reflected mirrored attribute. Plus declarative shadow DOM, no script.

    The preview starts the camera and owns its state (:state(live)); the pickers list real devices of their kind and follow devicechange.
