The same bar chart, three times

Five numbers, drawn with HTML and CSS, with SVG, and with canvas. Open DevTools → Elements and look inside each one: the HTML and the inline SVG are elements you can select, style and click; the canvas is one element, and what's drawn on it is a bitmap.
