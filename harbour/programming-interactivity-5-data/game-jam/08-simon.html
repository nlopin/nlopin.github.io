Game jam · spare

Simon

Four coloured pads. The game flashes a sequence; you repeat it. Every round adds one more step to the end. One wrong pad ends the game. ★★☆

“Green, red, red, blue, yellow… no, wait.”

You'll demo: round 1 flashes one pad; repeat it. Round 2 flashes the same pad and one more. Clicks during the flashing are ignored. A wrong pad: "Game over · you reached round 6".

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

State

  • The sequence, and how far into it the player has got.
  • Whose turn it is (the game's or the player's), and which pad is lit right now.

Render

  • Four pads drawn from state. The lit one is unmistakable.
  • The round number, and whose turn it is.

Rules

  • The game plays the sequence: one pad at a time, with a pause between flashes.
  • Clicks during playback are ignored.
  • The whole sequence right: one random step is added and it plays again. A wrong pad: game over.

The one rule: the DOM is output. Even the flashing: a lit pad is a value in state, drawn by render().

New tool: Code that runs later: setTimeout and setInterval. Not taught yet; MDN has what you need.

Edge cases (verify, minutes 35–42)

  • The same pad twice in a row: the player sees two flashes, not one long one.
  • Click pads while the sequence is playing.
  • Start a new game in the middle of playback.
  • Play with the keyboard: 1–4, or Tab and Enter.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • A tone per pad (Web Audio). Browsers only allow sound after the player has clicked something.
  • Playback gets faster every five rounds.
  • Everyone's stretch: save, best score. Does undo make sense here?

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first.

Hint 1

Write down the state at every moment of one playback of three steps. What changes it from one moment to the next?

Hint 2

The same pad twice in a row: what does the player see between the two flashes? Is that moment in your state?

Hint 3

When the player is halfway through repeating the sequence, what's the one number you need to check their next click?

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • New game during playback: two sequences play over each other.
  • The same pad twice shows up as one long flash.
  • The last pad of the sequence stays lit.

The 45 minutes

0–5 · planWrite the state on paper. What is stored, what is computed?
5–15 · renderA hand-written state draws the board. No clicks yet. At 15: change state in the console, call render(), the board follows.
15–35 · rulesEvents change the state. Win and lose last.
35–42 · verifyEvery edge case above. A new game mid-game.
42–45 · demoStop coding. Rehearse your 3 minutes once.

Presenting

Your 3 minutes
  1. Show the state first: type state in the console and walk through every key. Say what you chose not to store.
  2. Play it, and hit one edge case from your brief on purpose.
  3. One rule in code: the event, the state change, the render.
  4. One bug you hit, and how you found it.
