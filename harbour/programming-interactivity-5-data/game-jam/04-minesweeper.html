Game jam · game 4

Minesweeper

Ten mines hidden in a 9×9 board. Click a cell: a number says how many of its eight neighbours are mines, and a zero opens its neighbours by itself. Right-click to flag. Open every safe cell to win. ★★☆

“It said 1. I clicked the other one.”

You'll demo: the first click opens an area and is never a mine. Right-click flags a cell and the mines-left counter drops. Click a mine: every mine is shown and the board stops. Open every safe cell: you win.

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

State

  • For every cell: mine or not, opened or not, flagged or not.
  • Whether the game is on, lost or won.

Render

  • A 9×9 board drawn from state: hidden, flagged, opened with a number, opened and empty.
  • Mines left: mines minus flags.
  • Lost: every mine is shown, the one you hit stands out.

Rules

  • Click opens, right-click flags and unflags. A flagged cell can't be opened.
  • Opening a zero opens its neighbours, and so on.
  • The first click is never a mine.

The one rule: the DOM is output. Nothing in your game logic reads a cell's text or class to learn what's under it.

Edge cases (verify, minutes 35–42)

  • Ten first clicks in ten new games: none of them loses.
  • A zero in a corner, and a zero on an edge.
  • The numbers on edge and corner cells are right.
  • Right-click doesn't open the browser's menu.
  • Flag, click and right-click after the game is over.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • A timer that starts with the first click.
  • Chording: click an opened number whose flags are all placed, and its other neighbours open.
  • Phones have no right-click. A flag-mode switch, or a long press.
  • Difficulty levels: 9×9 / 10, 16×16 / 40.
  • Everyone's stretch: undo (is that cheating?), save, best time.

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first.

Hint 1

The number on an opened cell: do you need to store it?

Hint 2

A zero opens its neighbours, and their zeros open theirs. When does that stop? What keeps it from visiting the same cell again and again?

Hint 3

Do the mines have to exist before the first click?

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined, but only on the edges of the board.
  • Maximum call stack size exceeded on the first big opening.
  • Changing one cell changes a whole column.
  • A cell on the right edge counts mines from the left edge of the next row.

Presenting

Your 3 minutes
  1. Show the state first: type state in the console and walk through every key. Say what you chose not to store.
  2. Play it, and hit one edge case from your brief on purpose.
  3. One rule in code: the event, the state change, the render.
  4. One bug you hit, and how you found it.
