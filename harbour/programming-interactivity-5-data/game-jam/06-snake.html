Game jam · game 6

Snake

The snake moves one cell at a time on its own; the arrow keys turn it. Food makes it one cell longer. Hitting a wall or its own body ends the game. ★★★

“I was at 42 when my thumb slipped.”

You'll demo: the snake starts moving by itself. Steer it to the food: it grows and the score goes up. Turn straight back: nothing happens. Hit a wall: game over. New game: the same speed as the first one.

Must have (tick as you go)

What has to work, not how. The how is your call: you'll explain it on stage.

State

  • The cells the snake covers, and its direction.
  • Where the food is, and whether the game is over.

Render

  • A 20×20 board with the snake and the food drawn from state. The head looks different.
  • The score, and a game-over message.

Rules

  • Every tick (about 150 ms) the snake moves one cell.
  • Arrow keys turn it. Turning straight back is ignored.
  • Food: the snake grows by one, new food appears, never on the snake.
  • A wall or its own body: game over, the snake stops.

The one rule: the DOM is output. A collision is decided from state, never from what's drawn in a cell.

New tool: Code that runs again and again: setInterval. Not taught yet; MDN has what you need.

Edge cases (verify, minutes 35–42)

  • Moving right, press ←.
  • Moving right, press ↑ then ← very fast, faster than one tick.
  • Start three new games in a row: the speed stays the same.
  • Move the head into the cell the tail is just leaving.
  • The arrow keys don't scroll the page.

Stretch, if you're done early

  • Faster every five pieces of food.
  • Pause on Space, and automatically when you switch tabs.
  • A no-walls mode: leave on the right, come back on the left.
  • Everyone's stretch: undo (one tick back?), save, best score.

Stuck? One hint at a time

Try for five minutes first.

Hint 1

What does one tick do to the snake's cells? What is different about a tick where it eats?

Hint 2

Is "the direction the player last pressed" the same thing as "the direction the snake last moved"?

Hint 3

Do you need to redraw all 400 cells every tick, or only the things that exist? Either works: which is simpler for you?

Traps

Open when something behaves strangely
  • Every new game is faster than the last.
  • The snake turns back into itself, even though turning back is blocked.
  • The snake runs straight through its own body: no collision is ever found.
  • The game keeps going after game over, or a new game won't start.

15–35 · rulesEvents change the state. Win and lose last.
35–42 · verifyEvery edge case above. A new game mid-game.
42–45 · demoStop coding. Rehearse your 3 minutes once.

Presenting

Your 3 minutes
  1. Show the state first: type state in the console and walk through every key. Say what you chose not to store.
  2. Play it, and hit one edge case from your brief on purpose.
  3. One rule in code: the event, the state change, the render.
  4. One bug you hit, and how you found it.
