Exercise 6 · the debugger

Bug hunt

This little market has five bugs, reported by users below. For each one: reproduce it, put a breakpoint where you think it goes wrong (DevTools → Sources → app.js), look at the values, step until reality and your expectation part ways. Fix it in app.js, reload, press Run the checks: a report turns green when its test passes.

Rule of the game: no console.log. Breakpoints, the Scope panel and Watch only.

The checks run your app.js in hidden copies of this page, so your breakpoints would stop there too: deactivate breakpoints before you run them (Sources → the ⊘ button, or Ctrl/⌘ + F8), and untick Pause on caught exceptions.

Start with reports 3 and 5: they are the ones you can't solve by reading. Then 1, 2 and 4.

    Bug reports not checked yet

    1. “Food shows every stall, and now they're all food”

      Steps Click Food. Expected 3 food stalls. Actual all 8, and every tag says FOOD, even after All.

      Where to look

      A breakpoint inside the arrow function in visibleStalls. Click Food. Watch stall.tag before and after that line runs.

    2. “My night costs €142?”

      Steps Save Dumpling Dynasty (€8) and Taco Bike (€6), tip €2. Expected €16. Actual €142.

      Where to look

      Pause on the return in nightTotal. In the Scope panel, what type is each side of the +? (Hover them, or add typeof tip.value to Watch.)

    3. “It forgets everything when I reload”

      Steps Tick Cheapest first, save a stall (click the word “save”), reload. Expected both back. Actual unsorted, nothing saved. No error in the console.

      Where to look

      Is it saved at all? DevTools → Application → Local storage. Then: no error in the console doesn't mean no error. Sources → Breakpoints → tick Pause on caught exceptions, and reload.

    4. “The heart doesn't work, but the word ‘save’ does”

      Steps Click exactly on the ♡ (not the word). Expected the stall is saved. Actual nothing. Clicking the word “save” next to it works.

      Where to look

      A breakpoint on the first line of the list's click listener (or Event Listener Breakpoints → Mouse → click). Click the ♡. What is event.target? What did you mean?

    5. “The hearts are back, but it says ‘Save a stall’”

      Steps Needs report 3 fixed (otherwise nothing comes back at all). Save two stalls (click the word “save”), reload. Expected “2 saved · your night: …”. Actual two ♥, and the summary says “Save a stall to plan your night.”

      Where to look

      A breakpoint on the summary line in render, after a reload. What does the summary read, and what did load write? Hover both in the Scope panel.
